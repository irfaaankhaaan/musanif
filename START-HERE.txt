===========================================================================
  CONTENT ENGINE - START HERE
===========================================================================

A Slack bot you send a rough post to. It asks about the parts that need it,
then writes a finished LinkedIn post and an Instagram hook plus caption.


WHAT TO DO
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Double-click  setup.bat

     It installs everything and opens a file for your keys. It tells you
     what is wrong if anything is.

  2. Double-click  run-bot.bat

     Leave that black window open. Message the bot in Slack.

That is the whole thing.


YOU WILL NEED FOUR KEYS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  SLACK_BOT_TOKEN    from your Slack app, starts with  xoxb-
  SLACK_APP_TOKEN    from your Slack app, starts with  xapp-
  GROK_API_KEY       from console.x.ai, starts with    xai-
  ZERNIO_API_KEY     from your Zernio dashboard, starts with  sk_

  README.md has step-by-step pictures-in-words for making the Slack app.
  It is the only fiddly part, and you only do it once.

  GROK_API_KEY can be free. openrouter.ai/keys gives you a free Grok, and
  README.md shows the two extra lines to add if you use it.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Double-click  check-setup.bat

  It tests every part and says, in plain English, what to fix.
  Double-click  edit-secrets.bat  to correct a key.


THE FILES YOU WILL WANT TO EDIT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  voice.md     your writing rules. This is the one that changes the output.
  prompts/     the instruction files for writing and self-critique.

  Neither is code. Edit, restart the bot, the writing changes.


ON YOUR PHONE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Nothing to install. The bot is inside Slack, so it is already on the Slack
  app on your iPhone or Android. Open Slack, find it under Apps, and DM it.

  Finished posts are sent on their own, so long-pressing one and tapping
  Copy Text gives you exactly the post. Type  copy  to have them re-sent.

  But: the bot only answers while run-bot.bat is open on the computer. If
  that machine is asleep, messages from your phone wait until it wakes up.


NOTHING IS PUBLISHED UNTIL YOU SAY SO
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The bot starts in DRY RUN. It does all the work and shows you the posts
  but publishes nothing. Leave it that way until you trust it. To go live,
  run edit-secrets.bat and change the last line to  DRY_RUN=false
